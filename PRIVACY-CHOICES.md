# User Privacy Choices for Latte Dot

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Latte Dot (shown as **dot** in the app) stores its app data locally on the user's device. Latte Health Ltd does not receive or hold that data, so there is no server-side account or personal-data record for us to access, export, correct, or delete.

## Choose what to enter

Profile age and Medical ID text are optional. Users can leave these fields empty and can edit locally stored profile and medicine information in the app.

## Edit or remove individual records

Users can review and correct dose amounts and times in History. A mistaken dose record can be removed there. Medicines can be edited or archived; archiving removes a medicine from planning choices but keeps its existing history.

## Remove all app data

Deleting Latte Dot from the device removes the app's locally stored data from that device. Copies may remain in device backups controlled by the user or Apple. Backup inclusion, retention, restoration, and deletion are managed through the user's device and Apple account settings.

Because Latte Health Ltd does not receive the app's data, users do not need to submit a deletion request to us for data stored by Latte Dot.

## Analytics, advertising, and tracking

Latte Dot has no analytics, advertising, third-party tracking, or cloud sync. It does not use app data for targeted advertising or cross-app tracking, and there is no tracking or marketing-data consent to opt out of in the current version.

## Future changes

If a future version introduces accounts, data collection, analytics, advertising, tracking, or network services, this page and the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY.md) will be updated before that version is released.

## Questions

For privacy or support questions, open an issue in the [Latte Dot support repository](https://github.com/krlakshmikanth/dot/issues). GitHub issues are public, so do not include names, Medical ID information, medicine details, dose history, or other sensitive information.
