# Privacy Policy for Latte Dot

Last updated: 29 September 2026

Latte Dot (shown as **dot** in the app) is a local-first medication dose planner and logger published by Latte Health Ltd.

## Data stored by the app

The app may store the following information on the user's device:

- local profile names, ages, and optional Medical ID text entered by the user;
- medicine names, dose amounts, units, and user-entered dose limits;
- planned, confirmed, and uncertain dose records; and
- app preferences such as the selected profile and appearance.

This information is used only to provide the app's on-device features.

## Data collection and sharing

Latte Health Ltd does not collect, receive, sell, or share data from the app. The app has no account system, advertising, analytics, third-party tracking, cloud sync, or backend service.

The app does not transmit medication, profile, Medical ID, or dose-history data to Latte Health Ltd or to third parties. Device backups and other operating-system services are controlled separately by the user and Apple under their respective settings and policies.

## Data retention and deletion

App data remains on the user's device until it is edited or removed by the user, or until the app and its data are deleted from the device. Copies may remain in device backups managed by the user or the operating system.

## Children and family profiles

An adult may create local profiles for family members. Because the app does not transmit data to Latte Health Ltd, we do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

## Important health limitation

Latte Dot is not a dosing authority, drug database, interaction checker, clinical record, or emergency service. It evaluates only the limits entered by the user and the records stored on that device. Users should follow the medicine label and advice from a qualified clinician or pharmacist.

## Changes to this policy

If a future version introduces data collection or network services, this policy and the App Store privacy disclosure will be updated before that version is released.

## Contact

For privacy or support questions, open an issue in the [Latte Dot support repository](https://github.com/krlakshmikanth/dot/issues).
